Your usual smoothie

30 oz: banana, strawberries, mango, blueberries, approximately ⅓ cup whole A2 milk, ⅓ cup unsweetened almond milk, ⅓ cup water, and a 40 g premixed powder/seeds scoop.

Optional upgrade: ¼–½ cup rolled oats, and consider lower-fat A2 milk when convenient. Whole blended fruit preserves fiber better than strained juice.

Your blend contains quercetin and L-citrulline; review those supplements with a clinician rather than increasing the dose. Keep added pomegranate modest and favor seeds blended whole for fiber.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

Direct automatic syncing is not supported by a static GitHub Pages website. Apple Health data requires iPhone app authorization. This site supports manual entry and importing a CSV exported from an Apple Health export app or a spreadsheet.

Accepted CSV columns: date,type,value. Types: rhr, weight, steps, active, exercise, ldl, hdl, psa. For workouts use date,type,minutes,intensity with type walk, cardio, strength, or hiit. Each import file should contain only measurements or only workouts.

Private data backup

Your logs are stored in this browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to GitHub or a server. Browser data can be erased, so export a backup periodically. Another device will not automatically share the same data.

Privacy reminder: GitHub Pages hosts the website code, not your locally saved health logs. Do not commit exported health CSV/JSON files to a public GitHub repository.
